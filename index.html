一句话定位

治水学是一套面向复杂适应系统的存续分析元理论。它回答的核心问题是：系统如何在永恒流变中，通过动力与规则的协同，实现自身的存续。

治水学论纲·第零卷

大禹治水与存续域的知识形态——一种被遮蔽的认识论原型 第零卷·认识论部分 · Zenodo, 2026

摘要：以四千年前大禹治水为样本，显影一种被长期遮蔽的认识论原型：知识的最小单位不是命题，而是结构判断。鲧与禹的分野不是方法优劣，而是问题域的分叉——鲧在存在域追问“洪水是什么”，禹在存续域追问“洪水如何流”。大禹的“疏”，是存续域知识形态的第一次系统显影。

命题式与结构式：人类知识形态的另一种读法 第零卷·认识论部分 · 案例补充 · Zenodo, 2026

摘要：我们默认知识就是命题：最小单位是命题，为真的标准是符合实在，增长方式是提出、检验、修正。但人类在治水、农耕、中医、治国这类“如何继续下去”的问题上，一直在使用另一种知识——最小单位不是命题，而是对结构的判断；判据不是“对不对”，而是“能不能继续下去”。本文不另起一套说法，而是把治水学已有的“存在域／存续域”之分，换成更日常、更容易被跨学科读者接住的“命题式／结构式”，并通过符号系统与艺术形式两层并置，检验这个分叉在更大范围里是否仍站得住。

引用：宁凯峰. (2026). 命题式与结构式：人类知识形态的另一种读法（治水学论纲·第零卷·认识论部分·案例补充）. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23609363

AI治理（AI Governance）

Why AI Governance Must Be Dynamic: Pattern Continuity as the Ontological Ground for Diagnostic Governance SSRN Working Paper, 2026 · 治水学论纲·英文扩展篇 No. 1

Abstract:Current AI governance frameworks rely on a static-compliance paradigm, treating AI systems as discrete, bounded objects amenable to fixed classification and rule-based control. This paper argues that this paradigm rests on an ontological category mistake. Drawing upon Dynamic Sustenance Theory (DST), it proposes that AI systems are not static entities but pattern-continuous processes of sustenance: their identity persists not through unchanging components, but through the continuous, topologically recognizable coordination of forces and rules.

A General Structural Grammar for the Governance of Complex Systems: A Methodological Framework Zenodo, 2026 · 治水学论纲·英文扩展篇 No. 4

Abstract:Complexity science has produced powerful descriptive tools, yet it still lacks a shared structural language for intervention—a gap that lies not in method but in the problem domain. This paper argues that the proper question for the study of complex systems is not "what a system is" but "how a system sustains itself amid flux," and that governance is where this question becomes operational. AI governance, climate governance, financial stability, and urban emergency response repeatedly encounter the same kind of predicament—multi-agent self-adaptation, cross-level temporal asynchrony, and constraint structures that lose their adjustability under pressure—yet there is no common structural language for comparing and transferring lessons among them. This paper argues that the root of this predicament lies not in specific methods but in a triple misalignment among problem domain, epistemology, and methodology. The paper offers an operationalization of this alignment—a general structural grammar—comprising five basic constituents, a logical walkthrough from diagnosis to action (using platform governance as a simplified scenario), differential criteria against existing frameworks, and a four-rung validation ladder.

Citation:Ning, K.. (2026). A General Structural Grammar for the Governance of Complex Systems: A Methodological Framework. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23328739
