一句话定位

治水学是一套面向复杂适应系统的存续分析元理论。它回答的核心问题是：系统如何在永恒流变中，通过动力与规则的协同，实现自身的存续。

AI治理（AI Governance）

Why AI Governance Must Be Dynamic: Pattern Continuity as the Ontological Ground for Diagnostic Governance SSRN Working Paper, 2026 · 治水学论纲·英文扩展篇 No. 1

Abstract:Current AI governance frameworks rely on a static-compliance paradigm, treating AI systems as discrete, bounded objects amenable to fixed classification and rule-based control. This paper argues that this paradigm rests on an ontological category mistake. Drawing upon Dynamic Sustenance Theory (DST), it proposes that AI systems are not static entities but pattern-continuous processes of sustenance: their identity persists not through unchanging components, but through the continuous, topologically recognizable coordination of forces and rules.

Citation:Ning, K.. (2026). Why AI Governance Must Be Dynamic: Pattern Continuity as the Ontological Ground for Diagnostic Governance. SSRN. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7453298

Method, Not Stance: Structural Disclosure as an Operative Methodology for Human-AI Division of Labor Zenodo, 2026 · 治水学论纲·英文扩展篇 No. 2

Abstract:This paper presents structural disclosure (Xianying) as an operative methodology for human-AI division of labor. It replaces the vague human/AI boundary with a burden-bearing criterion: a judgment is non-delegable when a human occupies a pressure-bearing position and must bear the consequences of being wrong.

Citation:Ning, K.. (2026). Method, Not Stance: Structural Disclosure as an Operative Methodology for Human-AI Division of Labor. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22698811

Why Law Always Lags AI: A Dynamic Sustenance Theory Analysis of AI Governance Zenodo, 2026 · 治水学论纲·英文扩展篇 No. 3

The dynamic layer advances cumulatively on a month-to-quarter scale and cannot be paused; the rules layer proceeds through procedural cycles on a year-to-decade scale.
